# Preliminary startup-density proxy: undoped vs Al16-doped CoOOH

## Status

Candidate dataset. Suitable for mechanistic discussion, but not yet final manuscript data.

## Origin

Generated during previous Codex-assisted DFT analysis workflow.

## Purpose

This dataset visualizes charge and spin-density response between formal oxidation states/intermediates of pristine and Al-substituted CoOOH.

It is intended to support the hypothesis that Al substitution changes the charge/spin reorganization pathway during oxygen oxidation.

## Compared systems

- Undoped CoOOH
- Al16-doped CoOOH

## Shown quantities

- Charge response Δρ
- Spin response Δm
- Spin-up density response
- Spin-down density response

## Mechanistic relevance

The key question addressed by this analysis is whether pristine CoOOH requires stronger Co-centered charge/spin reconstruction than Al-substituted CoOOH during the oxidation process.

## Important limitations

This figure should not yet be interpreted as a final proof of oxidation-state assignment. It includes structural response and depends on the selected initial/final states and computational setup.

Further validation should include:

- consistency with cDFT results;
- integrated charge/spin analysis;
- orbital-resolved analysis;
- comparison of identical computational settings.
