# Current mechanism hypothesis

## Central hypothesis

Al substitution does not primarily change the oxygen oxidation event itself. Instead, it changes the available charge/spin accommodation pathway after oxygen oxidation.

Proposed sequence:

Al substitution
→ modified local electronic environment
→ altered charge/spin redistribution pathway
→ reduced Co-centered electronic reconstruction
→ alternative oxygen-related spin accommodation
→ easier *OH → *O conversion

## Pristine CoOOH

Supported observations:

- Oxygen-centered oxidation occurs.
- Oact loses charge during oxidation.
- Coact shows significant charge redistribution.
- Coact and Obridge show substantial spin response.

Supported interpretation:

The oxidized oxygen species is coupled to a broader Co-O framework. The system accommodates oxidation through distributed electronic and spin reconstruction.

## Al-substituted CoOOH

Supported observations:

- Oact oxidation remains comparable.
- Coact charge response is strongly reduced.
- Obridge spin response is strongly reduced.
- Oact exhibits spin-polarization reversal in cDFT/Hirshfeld and consistent Mulliken analysis.

Supported interpretation:

Al modifies the local magnetic/electronic boundary condition. The oxidized oxygen species can relax through a more localized O-centered spin accommodation pathway.

## Relation to experiment

Transient spectroscopy constraint:

Pristine CoOOH shows Co-related electronic response during the relevant reaction stage, while Al-substituted CoOOH shows strongly suppressed Co response.

Interpretation:

The reduced Co spectroscopic response is consistent with reduced Co-centered electronic reconstruction.

## Terminology guideline

Preferred:

- framework-coupled accommodation
- O-dominant spin accommodation
- charge/spin redistribution
- modified electronic boundary condition

Avoid unless directly proven:

- exact exchange energy stabilization
- direct electron transfer pathway
- single orbital spin flip mechanism
