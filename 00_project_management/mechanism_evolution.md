# Mechanism evolution

## Purpose

This document archives the evolution of the mechanistic interpretation for Al-substituted CoOOH OER calculations. It records how the current working model was formed and distinguishes observations from interpretations.

## Initial scientific question

Experiments indicate that Al substitution only slightly changes the first oxygen oxidation step, while substantially improving the *OH → *O conversion step. In addition, transient spectroscopy suggests that the Co-related photoinduced response observed in pristine CoOOH becomes strongly reduced or disappears after Al substitution.

The central question was therefore:

**Why does Al substitution selectively facilitate the *OH → *O transformation, and why does it suppress Co-centered electronic reconstruction?**

## Early hypotheses considered

### Hypothesis: Al simply changes adsorption energetics

Rejected as incomplete. The experimental selectivity between reaction steps cannot be explained only by a general electronic structure modification.

### Hypothesis: Al directly creates a new active site

Rejected as oversimplified. Current calculations suggest that Al is not the direct OER active center. Its role is to modify the electronic environment and available reconstruction pathways.

### Hypothesis: Al removes a magnetic/electronic constraint

Developed into the current working model. The important effect is not structural bond breaking but modification of the magnetic/electronic accommodation landscape around the oxidized oxygen species.

## Current evolution of the model

The interpretation evolved from a simple "Al breaks Co-O interaction" picture to a more precise model:

- Oxygen-centered oxidation occurs in both pristine and Al-substituted CoOOH.
- The amount of oxygen oxidation is comparable.
- The difference appears after oxidation, during self-consistent charge and spin redistribution.

## Current working model

Pristine CoOOH:

O-centered oxidation is accommodated by a collective response involving Co and neighboring oxygen framework.

Al-substituted CoOOH:

Replacement of a neighboring magnetic Co environment modifies the accessible spin/electronic accommodation pathway. Oxidized O can access a more localized O-centered spin-relaxed state with reduced Co charge reconstruction.

## Important avoided interpretations

The following statements should not be used as established conclusions:

- Al creates a new OER active site.
- Al breaks Al-O or Co-O structural bonds.
- A single electron spin flip causes the mechanism change.
- Al completely eliminates Co spin response.
- The exact magnetic exchange energy has been directly calculated.

These remain interpretations based on combined electronic structure evidence.
