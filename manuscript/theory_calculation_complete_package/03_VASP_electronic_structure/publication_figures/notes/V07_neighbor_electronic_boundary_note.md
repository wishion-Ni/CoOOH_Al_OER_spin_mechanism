# V07 - Neighbor-site electronic boundary introduced by Al substitution

## Quantity
Spin-resolved PDOS integrated over -2.0 to +0.5 eV relative to EF for the atom neighboring the active Co-O framework.

## Key result
- neighboring Co in pristine CoOOH: 0.764891 (spin up) + 1.684638 (spin down) = 2.449529 states
- neighboring Al in Al-substituted CoOOH: 0.004904 + 0.007538 = 0.012442 states

The total frontier-window weight is reduced by about 197-fold at the Al site.

## Mechanistic use
This supports describing Al as a frontier-inactive/nonmagnetic local electronic boundary that removes a neighboring Co-like frontier manifold. It helps rationalize why the local electronic/spin accommodation landscape differs after Al substitution.

## Complementary bonding context
The already-calculated framework bond rehybridization values are retained in `V07_framework_COHP_context.csv` but are not replotted here to avoid duplicating V01 COHP/ICOHP.

## Limitation
This descriptor alone does not prove the complete causal spin pathway; it should be interpreted together with the cDFT charge/spin response and validated Co-O bonding analysis.
