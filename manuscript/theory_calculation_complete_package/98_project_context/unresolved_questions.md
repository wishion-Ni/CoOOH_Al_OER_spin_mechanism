# Unresolved questions

This file separates open mechanistic questions from established observations and supported interpretations.

## Open question 1 — Why is the O-centered spin-relaxed state favored only after Al substitution?

Established observation:
- The Al-substituted system shows a strong Oact spin-polarization reversal in cDFT/Hirshfeld and Mulliken analyses.
- The pristine system does not show the same Oact spin reversal.

Supported interpretation:
- Al changes the local magnetic/electronic accommodation landscape.

Still unproven:
- A direct energy decomposition showing the energetic contribution of this spin reorganization.
- A rigorous counterfactual calculation of a pristine O-centered spin-reversed state and its energy.

## Open question 2 — What is the exact orbital pathway?

Established observation:
- Near-EF O-rich spin-polarized frontier states exist.
- Neighbor-Co magnetic d-like continuation is present in pristine CoOOH and largely absent at Al.
- Spin-resolved COHP shows that the Al-sensitive change is concentrated in one Co-O rebonding channel.

Still unproven:
- Unique identification of a single local orbital as the directly oxidized orbital.
- Exact assignment of global-axis dxy/dxz/dz2 projections to local Co-O-axis orbitals.
- Exact emitted-electron spin in the final cDFT-primary mechanism.

## Open question 3 — What precisely connects Al substitution to the altered O-state preference?

Supported interpretation:
- Al acts as a nonmagnetic electronic boundary that changes the Coact-Obridge-neighbor accommodation manifold.

Still unproven:
- A computed exchange parameter J.
- A quantitative magnetic-interaction energy decomposition.
- Whether the most appropriate theoretical language is exchange constraint, spin frustration, ligand-field reordering, or another many-body description.

## Open question 4 — Relation to spectroscopy

Established observation:
- Co-sensitive spectroscopic response changes strongly in pristine CoOOH and is suppressed after Al substitution.

Supported interpretation:
- This is consistent with reduced Co-centered electronic reconstruction after Al substitution.

Still open:
- Quantitative simulation of the transient spectrum from the calculated electronic states.
- Exact microscopic mapping from a specific spectral feature to a specific cDFT/DFT population change.

## Open question 5 — Thermodynamic causality

Established observation:
- Al substitution selectively lowers the *OH → *O thermodynamic step while the first OER step becomes slightly less favorable.

Supported interpretation:
- The benefit is intermediate-specific and is consistent with altered post-oxidation spin/electronic accommodation.

Still unproven:
- A strict decomposition of ΔG into charge-transfer, spin-reorganization, lattice, and bonding contributions.
