# cDFT evidence: Al redirects the *OH → *O spin response

## Dataset status

Candidate computational dataset.

This dataset is considered usable for mechanism development but is not yet designated as the final publication dataset.

## Origin

Generated during cDFT analysis workflow developed in Codex conversations.

The figure summarizes constrained DFT analysis of the *OH → *O conversion comparing undoped CoOOH and Al16-doped CoOOH active sites.

## Scientific purpose

The purpose of this dataset is to examine whether Al substitution changes the charge and spin reorganization pathway associated with oxygen oxidation.

The key mechanistic question is:

Why does pristine CoOOH exhibit strong Co-centered electronic reconstruction during *OH → *O, whereas Al substitution suppresses this response and enables a more favorable reaction pathway?

## Main observations represented in the figure

### Charge redistribution

Undoped system:

- Active Co shows significant charge redistribution.
- Adsorbate oxygen also undergoes strong charge change.

Al-substituted system:

- Active Co charge response is strongly reduced.
- Oxygen response remains comparable.

Interpretation:

Al substitution buffers the Co-centered charge response during oxidation.

### Local spin evolution

Undoped:

- Co local spin decreases substantially during *OH → *O.
- Indicates strong Co-centered spin reorganization.

Al-substituted:

- Co spin response is reduced.
- Oxygen spin response shows a distinct change including sign reversal behavior.

Interpretation:

Al substitution redirects the spin response from a Co-dominated pathway toward an alternative oxygen-involved pathway.

### Constrained-state fingerprints

The constrained calculations indicate different electronic requirements for generating the *O state.

These results should be interpreted together with free-energy calculations and experimental TAS observations.

## Connection to experimental constraints

Related experimental observation:

- Pristine CoOOH shows Co-associated TAS evolution during oxidation.
- Al-containing CoOOH suppresses this Co response.

This cDFT dataset provides a possible microscopic explanation:

- pristine CoOOH requires Co electronic/spin reconstruction;
- Al substitution avoids large Co reconstruction by accessing a different spin-adaptive oxidation pathway.

## Limitations

This dataset alone does not prove:

- absolute oxidation states;
- unique reaction mechanism;
- direct equivalence between constrained states and experimental intermediates.

Further correlation is required with:

- OER free-energy profile;
- COHP/orbital analysis;
- Bader charge;
- charge-density difference;
- spin density analysis.
