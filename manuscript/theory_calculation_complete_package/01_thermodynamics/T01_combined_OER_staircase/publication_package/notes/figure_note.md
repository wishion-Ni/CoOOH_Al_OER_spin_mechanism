# T01 figure note

## Scientific purpose

Compare the selected OER staircases for pristine CoOOH (Co32), the Al16 neighboring Co7 site, and the Al16 Al47 control site. This single panel is intended to carry the complete reaction-energy comparison; separate T02/T03 derivative plots are intentionally not produced because they add little independent information.

## Selected step energies (eV)

| System | * -> *OH | *OH -> *O | *O -> *OOH | *OOH -> O2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pristine Co32 | 1.199993 | 1.793143 | 1.534211 | 0.392652 |
| Al16 neighboring Co7 | 1.403097 | 1.524802 | 0.871955 | 1.120146 |
| Al16 Al47 control | 2.382189 | -1.011081 | 2.217542 | 1.331350 |

For pristine -> Al-neighboring-Co, the first step increases by +0.203104 eV and the *OH -> *O step decreases by 0.268341 eV.

## Figure design

- standalone subfigure size: 3.50 x 3.00 in (~89 x 76 mm);
- four-sided frame;
- horizontal state levels aligned for all systems;
- dashed connectors between intermediates;
- each elementary-step energy change labeled directly;
- no in-panel title or final panel lettering;
- palette may be adjusted in the final composite figure.

## Interpretation boundary

The archived source explicitly identifies this as the historical CP2K electronic-energy comparison without vibrational correction. The publication output is therefore labeled **Relative electronic energy (eV)** rather than strict Gibbs free energy. It must not be silently relabeled as the later 298.15 K CHE series.
