# Computational Methods

## Density-functional-theory calculations and structural models

Spin-polarized density-functional-theory calculations were performed with the Quickstep module of CP2K using the Perdew–Burke–Ernzerhof (PBE) generalized-gradient approximation. Goedecker–Teter–Hutter pseudopotentials and MOLOPT Gaussian basis sets were employed, with `TZVP-MOLOPT-PBE-GTH-q17`, `TZVP-MOLOPT-PBE-GTH-q6`, and `TZVP-MOLOPT-PBE-GTH-q1` for Co, O, and H, respectively. The Co 3d states were treated using an effective Hubbard interaction of U−J = 0.1212728 Hartree (3.30 eV). The auxiliary plane-wave density cutoff and relative cutoff were 400 and 55 Ry, respectively. Electronic minimization used the orbital-transformation method with a conjugate-gradient minimizer and `FULL_SINGLE_INVERSE` preconditioning. The inner and outer self-consistent-field convergence thresholds were 1 × 10−7 and approximately 1 × 10−6, respectively.

Pristine and Al-substituted CoOOH were described using the same periodic orthorhombic supercell (a = 8.6482 Å, b = 26.0793 Å, and c = 21.6574 Å). The pristine *OH model had the composition Co48H60O114, whereas the Al-substituted model had the composition Co40Al8H60O114. The corresponding *O structures were generated by removal of the adsorbate proton. All calculations were performed without imposing additional crystallographic symmetry.

## OER energetics and constrained DFT

The oxygen-evolution pathway was described by the conventional sequence * → *OH → *O → *OOH → O2. Relative electronic energies of the intermediates were obtained from the corresponding spin-polarized CP2K calculations and used to compare the reaction energetics at pristine Co sites, Co sites adjacent to Al substitution, and Al-site controls.

Constrained-density-functional-theory (cDFT) calculations were performed within the same CP2K framework to probe the localization and redistribution of charge and spin during the OER intermediates. Charge constraints were applied either to the active Co center or to a combined active-Co/adsorbate fragment, using N−1 and N+1 perturbations. Selected local-spin constraints were additionally applied to the *O state. The energy difference between a constrained solution and its corresponding unconstrained solution was used as the cDFT constraint penalty.

Local electron populations and spin moments were evaluated using both Hirshfeld and Mulliken partitioning. Reaction-induced changes were defined as the value for *O minus that for *OH. Spin-resolved population changes were further decomposed into α- and β-electron contributions.

## Reaction-induced charge and spin redistribution

Complementary fixed-geometry electronic-structure calculations were performed for pristine-*OH, pristine-*O, Al-substituted-*OH, and Al-substituted-*O states using VASP. To isolate the electronic redistribution associated with the *OH → *O oxidation step from structural relaxation, the *O electronic density was evaluated at the corresponding *OH geometry. The reaction-induced charge-density difference was therefore defined as

Δρ = ρ(*O; R*OH) − ρ(*OH; R*OH),

where R*OH denotes the *OH geometry. The magnetization-density difference, Δm, was evaluated analogously. With m = ρ↑ − ρ↓, the spin-resolved density differences were obtained from

Δρ↑ = 1/2(Δρ + Δm),

Δρ↓ = 1/2(Δρ − Δm).

The same local plane containing the active Co, adsorbate O, bridge O, and neighboring Co/Al site was used to compare the pristine and Al-substituted systems.

## Projected density of states and Bader analysis

Site- and orbital-projected densities of states (PDOS) were extracted for the active Co, adsorbate O, bridge O, and neighboring metal site. Energies were referenced to the Fermi level of each calculation. The active-Co d-state centroid was calculated as

εd = ∫Eρd(E)dE / ∫ρd(E)dE,

using the occupied Co 3d states from −8 to 0 eV relative to the Fermi level. Spin-resolved centroids were evaluated analogously.

Bader charge analysis was performed for the matched fixed-geometry VASP calculations. Changes in Bader electron population for the *OH → *O step were evaluated for the active Co, adsorbate O, bridge O, and neighboring Co/Al site using the same atom mapping between the two states.

## Chemical-bonding analysis

Chemical-bonding analysis was carried out with LOBSTER 5.1.1 using the `pbeVaspFit2015` basis set. Projected crystal orbital Hamilton population (pCOHP) and integrated COHP (ICOHP) analyses were performed for the relevant Co–O interactions. A Gaussian smearing width of 0.05 eV and an energy range from −15 to +5 eV were used. Bond pairs were assigned from the relaxed structures and cross-checked against the corresponding interatomic distances. Native pCOHP signs were retained during data extraction; figures plotted as −pCOHP or −ICOHP use the corresponding sign convention explicitly. LOBSTER Mulliken and Löwdin orbital populations were additionally used to examine spin-selective redistribution during the *OH → *O step.
