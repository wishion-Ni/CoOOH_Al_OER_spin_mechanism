# Theory Figure Export Standards

## Layout

- Design at intended subfigure size, not full multi-panel-figure width.
- Typical standalone computational panel width: ~85-90 mm.
- Preserve readable text after placement into the final composite figure.
- Do not add final manuscript panel letters; collaborators control final assembly.
- Keep all four plot borders for conventional quantitative data plots.

## Typography

- Sans-serif publication font; current scripts use `Liberation Sans` as an open substitute for Arial/Helvetica.
- Typical axis labels: ~8.5-9 pt at exported panel size.
- Tick labels: ~7.5-8 pt.
- Legends/annotations: ~6.5-8 pt depending on density.
- Avoid a figure title inside a manuscript panel.

## Lines and annotations

- Quantitative line widths should remain clear after reduction (~1-2 pt at final panel scale).
- Use visually coherent colors; color identity is not permanently tied to a system unless later required by the assembled manuscript.
- Direct numerical annotations are appropriate when they materially improve comparison and do not create clutter.

## Export formats

For conventional plots provide PDF, SVG, EPS when practical, PNG at 600 dpi, and TIFF at 600 dpi. Also provide source CSV and plotting scripts so collaborators can redraw the panel in Origin, Python, MATLAB or other software.

## 3D electronic-density images

For charge/spin-density or orbital isosurfaces, provide high-resolution PNG/TIFF plus source volumetric data, isovalue, color meaning, view direction, atom-display/cropping choices, and rendering software/version when known.
