# Experimental Constraints

The calculation archive must address these observations:

1. Al substitution lowers the energetic cost of the *OH -> *O OER step.
2. TAS indicates Co electronic/species evolution in pristine CoOOH.
3. After Al substitution, the Co response is suppressed.
4. The first OER step increases slightly, approximately 0.2 eV.
5. For pristine CoOOH, `s2+s3` is approximately 3.2 eV and `s2` is rate determining.

The computational narrative must therefore explain a step-specific response, not merely state that Al changes the electronic structure. The central test is whether Al enables an alternative O-rich/spin-adaptive response while reducing the need for Co-centered reconstruction during *OH -> *O.
