# Metadata: cDFT Charge/Spin Mechanism Dataset

- **Status:** candidate for mechanism discussion; supporting for quantitative charge claims.
- **Source:** completed undoped cDFT baseline and matched Al16/undoped mechanism validation bundles.
- **Method:** CP2K constrained DFT with population/spin analysis; local populations are method dependent.
- **Scientific purpose:** test whether pristine CoOOH accommodates *OH -> *O oxidation through stronger Co-centered charge/spin reconstruction than Al-substituted CoOOH.
- **Key observation:** the preferred interpretation is suppressed Co-centered reconstruction after Al substitution with a more O-rich spin response.
- **Limitations:** constrained branches, population partitioning, missing canonical frontier eigenvalue/orbital projections in some CP2K WFN records, and method-dependent spin labels.
- **Related datasets:** VASP Bader/PDOS/LOBSTER in `04_VASP_analysis`.
