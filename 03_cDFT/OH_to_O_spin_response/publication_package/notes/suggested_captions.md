## C01
Reaction-induced local electron-population response between the selected *OH and *O states at the active Co and adsorbate O sites. Filled circles and open diamonds denote Hirshfeld and Mulliken partitions, respectively. Al substitution strongly suppresses the Co-centered population response while substantial O-centered oxidation response remains.

## C02
Reaction-induced local spin response between the selected *OH and *O states at the active Co and adsorbate O sites. Filled circles and open diamonds denote Hirshfeld and Mulliken partitions, respectively. Relative to pristine CoOOH, Al substitution reduces the Co-centered spin response and is accompanied by a stronger O-centered spin reorganization.
