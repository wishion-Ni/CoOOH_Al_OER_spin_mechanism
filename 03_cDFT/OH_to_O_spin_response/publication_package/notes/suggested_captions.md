# Suggested captions

**C01 — Charge redistribution.** Reaction-induced local electron-population changes between the selected *OH and *O electronic states. Filled circles and open diamonds denote Hirshfeld and Mulliken partitions, respectively. Al substitution strongly suppresses the active-Co population response while substantial adsorbate-O oxidation response remains.

**C02 — Local-spin evolution.** Hirshfeld local spin moments for *OH (open symbols) and *O (filled symbols). Pristine CoOOH shows a larger active-Co spin decrease, whereas the Al-substituted system shows a reduced Co response together with stronger adsorbate-O spin reorganization.

**C03 — Spin-change partition.** Hirshfeld spin-resolved population changes, ΔNα and ΔNβ, for the active Co and adsorbate O during *OH → *O. The panel resolves the local spin response into its spin-channel population components.

**C04 — Constrained-state fingerprints for *O.** Valid cDFT localization/spin constraint penalties for the selected *O states. Co-only and Co+adsorbate N±1 constraints probe charge localization, while the Co-only M+2 constraint probes a Co-centered spin accommodation channel. The rejected Al16 M−2 branch is omitted. These quantities are cDFT constraint penalties and are not CHE free energies or kinetic barriers.
